MEETING SUMMARIZER EXAMPLES
===========================

These examples show how to call summarize_meeting() directly
or trigger it through the interactive CLI.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Example 1 — Marketing strategy meeting
--------------------------------------------------------------
notes = """
Reviewed Q2 marketing results. Lead generation was down 15% vs Q1.
Root cause identified as delayed ad creatives and paused LinkedIn campaigns.

Team agreed to increase Q3 marketing budget from PKR 400,000 to PKR 550,000.
Fatima assigned as Q3 campaign lead. She must submit a campaign brief by May 30.
Usman from design to deliver 3 ad creatives per channel per month.
HR to post a content writer job listing by end of this week.

Decision to stop Google Display Ads and shift full budget to LinkedIn and email marketing.
Next meeting scheduled for June 5th at 10am.
"""

assistant.summarize_meeting(
    notes=notes,
    date="May 22, 2026",
    attendees=["Fatima (Marketing Lead)", "Usman (Design)", "HR Manager", "CEO"]
)

Expected output:
    ===========================================
    MEETING SUMMARY
    ===========================================
    Date      : May 22, 2026
    Attendees : Fatima (Marketing Lead), Usman (Design), HR Manager, CEO

    SUMMARY
    The team reviewed Q2 marketing performance and approved a budget increase
    for Q3 to address lead generation shortfalls caused by delayed creatives.

    KEY DISCUSSION POINTS
    - Q2 lead generation down 15% due to paused campaigns
    - Q3 budget increase from PKR 400k to PKR 550k approved
    - Ad creative production workflow to be improved

    DECISIONS MADE
    1. Q3 marketing budget set at PKR 550,000
    2. Google Display Ads discontinued
    3. Full budget shifted to LinkedIn and email marketing

    ACTION ITEMS
    - Fatima: Submit Q3 campaign brief by May 30
    - Usman: Deliver 3 ad creatives per channel per month
    - HR: Post content writer job listing by end of this week

    NEXT MEETING: June 5, 2026 at 10:00 AM
    ===========================================

--------------------------------------------------------------
Example 2 — Product roadmap meeting
--------------------------------------------------------------
notes = """
Discussed Q3 product priorities. Team agreed to focus on mobile app
over web dashboard due to user feedback showing 70% mobile usage.
Ahmed to deliver UI mockups by May 30. Sana to coordinate with the
dev team on sprint planning. Q3 budget approved at PKR 800,000.
No blockers identified. Next sync on June 1st.
"""

assistant.summarize_meeting(
    notes=notes,
    date="May 20, 2026",
    attendees=["Ahmed (Design)", "Sana (PM)", "Dev Team Lead", "CTO"]
)

--------------------------------------------------------------
Example 3 — Client onboarding meeting (no attendees specified)
--------------------------------------------------------------
notes = """
Kicked off the NexaTech ERP project. Scope confirmed: accounting,
HR, inventory, and reporting modules. Go-live date set for September 1.
Client to provide data migration files by June 15. Our team starts
development sprint on June 1. Weekly check-ins every Monday at 2pm.
Project manager assigned: Bilal.
"""

assistant.summarize_meeting(
    notes=notes,
    date="May 22, 2026"
    # attendees not required
)

--------------------------------------------------------------
CLI usage
--------------------------------------------------------------
Select option: 3
  Paste meeting notes. Type END on a new line when done.

  > Discussed Q3 strategy. Budget approved at 500k.
  > Sara to lead the campaign. Launch June 1.
  > END

  Meeting date [today]: May 22, 2026
  Attendees, comma-separated: Sara, Ahmed, Finance Team
