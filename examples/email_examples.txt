EMAIL WRITER EXAMPLES
=====================

These examples show how to call the write_email() method directly
or trigger it through the interactive CLI.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Example 1 — Formal stakeholder email (no research)
--------------------------------------------------------------
assistant.write_email(
    purpose="Announce Q2 2026 sales results. Total revenue was PKR 12.5 million, "
            "a 28% increase compared to Q1 2026.",
    recipient="stakeholder",
    tone="formal"
)

Expected output structure:
    Subject: Q2 2026 Sales Performance Update

    Dear [Stakeholder Name],

    I am pleased to share our Q2 2026 performance results...
    [3-4 paragraphs covering results, context, and outlook]

    Best regards,
    [Your Name]
    CEO, [Company Name]

--------------------------------------------------------------
Example 2 — Friendly client email with market research
--------------------------------------------------------------
assistant.write_email(
    purpose="Introduce our new AI-powered inventory management software "
            "to potential retail clients.",
    recipient="client",
    tone="friendly",
    research_topic="AI adoption in retail and supply chain 2026"
)

What happens internally:
    1. web_search("AI adoption in retail and supply chain 2026") is called
    2. Relevant statistics are incorporated into the email body
    3. Full email is returned with research-backed content

--------------------------------------------------------------
